Enhance the Booking Form and Booking Flow with the following requirements:

1. Guest Details Enhancement
   In the guest section, capture detailed guest counts including:

* Total Adults
* Total Kids (Below Age 10)
* Male Count
* Female Count

The male/female count should include both adults and children.

2. Business Details (Optional)
   In both:

* Website Booking Form
* Scheduler/Admin Booking View

Add optional fields:

* Business Name
* GST Number

If business details are provided:

* The invoice should be generated using the Business Name.
* GST details should be included in the invoice.

3. Purpose of Visit (Optional for Everyone)
   Add an optional dropdown field:

* Purpose of Visit

Options:

* Family Function
* Leisure
* Corporate Business Visit
* Other

If "Other" is selected:

* Show a manual text input field for custom entry.

This field should be available for all bookings.

4. Payment Method Option
   Add Payment Method selection in:

* Website Booking Flow
* Scheduler/Admin Booking View

Examples:

* Online Payment
* Cash
* UPI
* Card
* Bank Transfer
* Pay Later

5. Website Booking Payment Flow Enhancement
   Currently, the website redirects users to Razorpay payment immediately on clicking Book.

Enhance this flow by adding another option:

* "Pay Later"

Flow for Pay Later:

* The customer can submit the room booking request without making payment immediately.
* The booking request should be sent to the admin for approval.
* The room should remain in pending/request status until approved by admin.

6. Razorpay Advance Payment Logic
   When the user selects Online Payment (Razorpay):

* Do not charge the full booking amount.
* Only 50% of the total booking amount should be charged as advance payment.
* Clearly display to the customer that only advance payment is being collected online.
* Remaining payment can be collected later during check-in or final settlement.

7. Admin Booking Confirmation Enhancement
   The booking confirmation feature already exists. Enhance it with the following:

While approving/confirming a booking request, the admin should be able to:

* Enter Amount Paid
* Select Payment Method
* Update Final Room Price after negotiation/bargaining
* Confirm whether payment is Partial or Full

After confirmation:

* The booking status should be updated accordingly.
* Payment and pricing details should be saved properly.

8. General Requirements

* Keep the UI clean and user-friendly.
* Use conditional fields wherever required.
* Ensure all new fields are available in both frontend and admin/scheduler views.
* Ensure booking, invoice, payment, and approval data are properly stored in the database.
